# EVT1 mechanical release note

The POWER1 extension is an integral part of the V2 PCB, not a separate
board. EVT1 replaces the empty oversized MicroClasp-era geometry with a
filleted 11.0 mm wide by 10.0 mm projection sized for Molex 105311-1202.

The final edge-centerline envelope is 40.575 x 50.500 mm. The 0.075 mm width
spread is inherited from the existing nominal 40.5 mm main-body edge
coordinates; that body and its 30.5 x 30.5 mm M3 pattern were not moved.

The exact 105311-1202 vendor STEP is associated with the custom footprint and
appears in the board STEP/renders. Pads, solder clips, locator, latch direction,
mating envelope and 3 mm release-side access are documented. No mouse bites are
used between tab and body.
