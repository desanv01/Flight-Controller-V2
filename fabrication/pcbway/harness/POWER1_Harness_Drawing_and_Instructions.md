# POWER1 harness drawing and build instructions

Board mating housing Molex 105307-1202; two gold female terminals 105300-2200; TPA 105325-1002. Use 22-AWG stranded thin-wall copper, insulation OD ≤1.60 mm. Cut red and black conductors to 100 mm target (150 mm EVT1 maximum). Strip only to the Molex applicator/hand-tool specification; use an approved crimp tool and inspect conductor/insulation crimps. Pin 1 red = regulated +5V; pin 2 black = GND. Keep ≥12.7 mm free wire after housing before tie/bend. BEC end may be soldered to Matek BEC12S-PRO output pads with heat-shrink and strain relief. Label `REGULATED 5.2V ONLY — NO VBAT`. No hot-plugging.

Mating-face view: `[ 1 +5V RED ][ 2 GND BLACK ]`. Wire-entry view is mirrored: `[ 2 BLACK ][ 1 RED ]`. Verify cavity numbers molded into housing before crimp insertion.
