# J12 sourcing and THT instructions

**Populate J12 with exact Molex 105311-1202 only. Black, 0.38 µm gold version. No substitutes. No tin-contact version. Customer approval required before any sourcing or part-number change.**

Top-side insertion; pin 1 is `+5V`, pin 2 is `GND`; latch faces the tab free edge. Inspect pin-1 orientation, both electrical PTH joints and solder-clip wetting. Preferred sourcing sequence: (1) PCBWay turnkey from an authorized distributor; (2) customer-consigned exact part; (3) DNP only if exact sourcing/consignment is impossible, for controlled hand soldering after assembly. Housing 105307-1202, contacts 105300-2200 and TPA 105325-1002 are harness parts and must not be added to the board BOM.
